# Product Roadmap Visual Prompts

For the productization visuals. A **timeline** system — draw in a design tool with the tokens, or
generate an abstract timeline background and label it.

**Shared tokens:** background #071018, grid #29384C, gold #D5B56E for the active node + accents,
#29384C connector line, muted outlined future nodes, cream labels, mono phase numbers, blue #7CB4FF
for value/data callouts, green #8EE0B2 for "shipped".

---

## Visual A — The 6-phase ramp (hero timeline)
A slim horizontal timeline, 6 nodes evenly spaced:
- **P0 · Open proof-of-work** — filled gold, `● live`, green "shipped" tick
- **P1 · Gated beta** — accounts, saved sessions
- **P2 · Personal layer** — saved resumes, watchlists, alerts
- **P3 · Coach dashboard** — multi-client management
- **P4 · Institution** — schools, workforce, cohorts
- **P5 · Market expansion** — new cities, sectors
- **P6 · Data / API** — endpoints, B2B intelligence
Connector line in #29384C; P0 filled gold with a "YOU ARE HERE" chip; P1–P6 outlined and muted so the
progression reads instantly. One ghosted gold orb behind. Generous spacing.

## Visual B — Value ladder (who pays for what)
A vertical staircase of 6 steps rising left-to-right. Each step labeled with **phase · payer · value**:
`P2 individuals · memory+alerts` → `P3 coaches · manage clients` → `P4 institutions · cohort outcomes`
→ `P6 businesses · grounded intelligence`. The higher the step, the more gold. Caption:
"The dashboard is the wedge. The data layer is the moat."

## Visual C — Wedge & moat
Two abstract shapes: a **gold wedge** (labeled "Dashboard — the wedge") pointing into a market, and a
**blue shield/moat outline** (labeled "Data layer — the moat") behind it. Minimal, conceptual, on the
grid. This is a strong standalone graphic for the business-model post.

## Visual D — Market-configurable engine
A single central "engine" card with a small **config file chip** feeding it, and three faint city
outlines (Toronto lit gold; two others muted "next") radiating out. Caption:
"One config retargets the whole system." Communicates scale without a rebuild.

## Visual E — Maturity states (before → after)
Two panels: **"Dashboard"** (a lone card, `● live`, no users) → **"Product"** (same card now ringed by
user nodes, a workflow loop, a trust badge, and a `$` chip). Caption: "Users · workflows · trust ·
business model." The literal definition of the productization thesis.

---
**Timeline layout note:** keep the whole ramp on one slide/section when possible — the *shape* of the
progression is the message. If it must wrap, break at P3/P4 (the B2C→B2B seam) and label the seam.
