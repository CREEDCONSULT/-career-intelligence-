# Carousel 1 — "This is not a dashboard demo. It is a proof-of-work system."

**Purpose:** position the project as proof of AI product strategy.
**Slides:** 9 · **Size:** 1080×1350 · **System:** dark dashboard (see `/design_system`).
Format per slide → `EYEBROW` / **Headline** / body / *design note*.

---

### Slide 1 — Cover
`PROOF-OF-WORK · 01`
**This is not a dashboard demo.**
**It is a proof-of-work system.**
Career Intelligence Dashboard — how I build with AI, not just prompt it.
*Design: grid overlay, one ghosted gold orb top-right, thin gold divider under the title. A small "● live" pill bottom-left.*

### Slide 2 — The real question
`THE QUESTION`
**The question wasn't "can AI give career advice?"**
It was: *how do I use AI to make better decisions — and prove the answer is trustworthy?*
*Design: large cream question text, muted-slate follow line. Empty space. Let it breathe.*

### Slide 3 — The problem
`THE PROBLEM`
**Career advice is generic, ungrounded, and outdated.**
"Learn in-demand skills." Which ones? What city? Growing or fading? Paying what?
Most advice can't say. It runs on vibes, not data.
*Design: three faded chip tags — "generic" "ungrounded" "outdated" — in muted red outline.*

### Slide 4 — The design choice
`DESIGN PRINCIPLE`
**Data first. AI second. UX third.**
The order is the strategy. A model on top of bad data is just a confident way to be wrong.
*Design: three stacked numbered cards (01 Data / 02 AI / 03 UX) with gold stage markers.*

### Slide 5 — The system
`ARCHITECTURE`
**A pipeline, not a prompt.**
Labour-market data → analytics → guarded AI workflows → career decisions.
*Design: horizontal flow with 4 blocks and gold arrows. Blue accent on the AI block, green tick on the output.*

### Slide 6 — The product thinking
`THE PRINCIPLE`
**The model is not the source of truth.**
**The system is.**
The AI explains and guides. The data decides. That boundary is the product.
*Design: two contrasting cards — "Model: translates" (blue) vs "System: decides" (gold), divider between.*

### Slide 7 — The proof
`RECEIPTS`
**A working system, not a pitch.**
→ Live app anyone can open
→ Public repo anyone can read
→ 97,227 postings · 8 modules · 100 tests
→ Real evaluation numbers, limitations included
*Design: 2×2 metric tiles. Monospace figures in cream, gold labels.*

### Slide 8 — The bigger lesson
`THE LESSON`
**AI strategy becomes real when it becomes a system.**
Anyone can demo a model. The work is designing the layers that make it trustworthy.
*Design: single bold statement, centred, gold underline. Ghosted orb behind.*

### Slide 9 — CTA
`WHAT'S NEXT`
**I'll break down the build in stages.**
Data. AI. Guardrails. UX. Scale.
Live app + repo in the comments. Follow along.
— Mesomachukwu Mezie-Akabudu · creedConsult
*Design: clean sign-off card, small avatar/monogram, gold "Follow the build" pill.*
