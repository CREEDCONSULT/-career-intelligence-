# Carousel 4 — "From open dashboard to scalable product"

**Purpose:** show business and product strategy.
**Slides:** 10 · **Size:** 1080×1350 · **System:** dark dashboard.

---

### Slide 1 — Cover
`PRODUCTIZATION · 04`
**From open dashboard**
**to scalable product.**
The ramp I designed before I shipped step one.
*Design: grid overlay; a faint 6-node timeline runs behind the title, node 1 lit gold.*

### Slide 2 — Today
`PHASE 0 · NOW`
**Today: an open proof-of-work dashboard.**
Live, honest, and deliberately not monetized. Its job is credibility.
*Design: single "Phase 0" card, "● live" pill, gold border.*

### Slide 3 — Stage 1
`PHASE 1`
**Public demo → portfolio case study.**
Turn traffic into a story and a repeatable proof.
*Design: roadmap node 1 → 2 with gold connector; muted future nodes ahead.*

### Slide 4 — Stage 2
`PHASE 1.5 → 2`
**Gated beta with user profiles.**
Login, accounts, saved sessions, usage analytics. Anonymous traffic becomes known users.
*Design: a "login" tile + a small funnel graphic (visitors → users).*

### Slide 5 — Stage 3
`PHASE 2`
**Saved resumes, career plans, role tracking.**
The product stops being a lookup and becomes a companion that remembers you.
*Design: three feature tiles — Saved Resume · Watchlist · Skill-Gap Plan.*

### Slide 6 — Stage 4
`PHASE 3 → 4`
**Coach, school, and workforce dashboards.**
Multi-user management, client notes, cohort analytics. The first real B2B.
*Design: an org-tree motif — one admin node over many user nodes.*

### Slide 7 — Stage 5
`PHASE 5`
**Beyond Toronto.**
The engine is market-configurable — one config retargets the whole system. New cities, new sectors.
*Design: a faint map/grid with several city markers; "config → market" chip.*

### Slide 8 — Stage 6
`PHASE 6`
**Paid plans, API access, B2B intelligence.**
Sell the grounded intelligence, not just the interface.
*Design: an "API" endpoint card with three integration lines out to partner blocks.*

### Slide 9 — The product value
`THE VALUE`
**Trusted, updated, personalized career intelligence.**
The dashboard is the wedge. The clean data layer is the moat.
*Design: two labelled shapes — a wedge (gold) and a shield/moat (blue) — side by side.*

### Slide 10 — CTA
`THE TAKEAWAY`
**A dashboard becomes a product when it has users, workflows, trust, and a business model.**
I built the trust first. The rest is sequencing.
Roadmap + live app in the comments.
*Design: bold closing line, gold underline, monogram sign-off.*
