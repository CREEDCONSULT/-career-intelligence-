# Carousel 5 — "How I use AI to stay ahead"

**Purpose:** connect the project to my personal brand.
**Slides:** 10 · **Size:** 1080×1350 · **System:** dark dashboard.
*Best opener for Week 1 — it frames me before it frames the product.*

---

### Slide 1 — Cover
`OPERATING SYSTEM · 05`
**How I use AI**
**to stay ahead.**
Not a tool. A method.
*Design: grid overlay, one ghosted gold orb, confident single title. Minimal.*

### Slide 2 — Not just answers
`REFRAME`
**I don't just use AI for answers.**
Answers are cheap now. Everyone has them.
*Design: a faded "Q → A" motif, deliberately understated.*

### Slide 3 — Decision systems
`THE SHIFT`
**I use AI to build decision systems.**
The edge isn't the answer. It's the system that produces trustworthy answers on demand.
*Design: the "Q → A" motif upgraded into a small pipeline with a checkpoint.*

### Slide 4 — Example: careers
`APPLICATION · 01`
**Career decisions are one example.**
So I built a Career Intelligence Dashboard — grounded in 97,227 real Toronto postings.
*Design: a single dashboard tile screenshot-style, gold "01" marker.*

### Slide 5 — Example: research
`APPLICATION · 02`
**Business research is another.**
Deconstruct a market, structure the signals, let AI surface what matters.
*Design: same tile frame, "02" marker; a faint chart motif.*

### Slide 6 — Example: strategy
`APPLICATION · 03`
**Product strategy is another.**
Turn ambiguity into layers, roadmaps, and decisions you can defend.
*Design: "03" marker; a faint layered-stack motif.*

### Slide 7 — Example: content
`APPLICATION · 04`
**Content systems are another.**
This campaign is one. A system, not a scramble.
*Design: "04" marker; a faint calendar/grid motif.*

### Slide 8 — The common pattern
`THE METHOD`
**Deconstruct the system.**
**Build the workflow.**
**Automate the intelligence.**
Same three moves, any domain.
*Design: three numbered cards, gold markers, equal weight. The core of the whole deck.*

### Slide 9 — The artifact
`PROOF`
**The Career Intelligence Dashboard is one artifact of that operating system.**
You can click this one. The method runs quietly behind the rest.
*Design: the dashboard tile again, now labelled "artifact of the method".*

### Slide 10 — CTA
`WHAT'S NEXT`
**This is the kind of AI work I want to keep building.**
Systems that compound. Not tools I chase.
Live app + repo in the comments. Let's build.
*Design: closing line, gold underline, monogram sign-off, "Follow the build" pill.*
