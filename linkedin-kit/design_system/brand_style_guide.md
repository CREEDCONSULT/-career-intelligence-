# Brand Style Guide — Career Intelligence Campaign

The umbrella doc. Detailed specs live in `color_palette.md`, `typography_and_layout.md`,
`visual_motifs.md`, `slide_design_rules.md`. This is the "why + feel."

## Brand idea
**A strategic intelligence system — analytical, executive, and quietly premium.**
It should feel like the console of a research lab that also has product taste: dark, gridded,
data-native, calm. Not a startup. Not a cyberpunk fantasy. A serious instrument.

## Feels like
- Premium dashboard UI · dark-mode data product
- Modern AI operations console · research lab meets product strategy
- Clean, technical, executive — analytical but visually beautiful

## Does NOT feel like
- Generic AI content · robots or humanoid AI
- Neon cyberpunk / glitch / matrix · playful startup blobs
- Stock-photo people / handshakes · brain-with-chips clichés

## The three brand signals (must be present across assets)
1. **Grounding** — everything traces to data. Visually: cards cite sources, numbers are real, ticks mean verified.
2. **Systems** — layers, flows, pipelines. Visually: blocks connected by arrows, numbered stages, roadmaps.
3. **Restraint** — one accent leads, lots of space. The confidence is in what's left out.

## Signature elements (the "recognizable at a glance" kit)
- Deep navy background + faint technical grid.
- **Gold** as the single signature accent (labels, dividers, "you are here").
- Mono numbers and eyebrow labels.
- The **data → AI → decision** flow diagram.
- The trust **scorecard** (real eval numbers).
- The `PROOF-OF-WORK` / `● LIVE` / `GROUNDED` pills.

## Voice on visuals (microcopy)
- Eyebrow labels: `ARCHITECTURE · 02`, `GUARDRAILS · 03`, `PHASE 0 · NOW`.
- Trust marks: `GROUNDED`, `VERIFIED`, `SELECT-ONLY`, `● LIVE`.
- Never marketing-speak on a slide. Labels are technical and plain.

## Color meaning (semantic, not decorative)
- **Gold** = signature / key / active. **Blue** = data & AI. **Green** = verified/passing.
- **Red** = warning only (rare). **Cream** = say. **Slate** = support.

## Logo / identity treatment
- Use a simple **monogram** ("MMA" or "M·CIS") in cream, mono, on a dark chip — bottom-left sign-off.
- Pair with "Mesomachukwu Mezie-Akabudu · creedConsult" in slate.
- No emblem/mascot. The identity is the *system look*, not a logo.

## Consistency across formats
| Format | Keep constant |
|--------|---------------|
| Carousels | cover/closer template, eyebrow format, footer, palette |
| Text posts | header image (optional) uses the same dark card + gold divider |
| Landing / case study (HTML) | same tokens, cards, grid, gold accents |
| Screenshots | framed in dark card + `#29384C` border + mono caption |

## The one-line brand test
> If it could be a frame *inside* the Career Intelligence Dashboard, it's on-brand.
> If it looks like a stock "AI" graphic, it's off-brand. Delete it.
