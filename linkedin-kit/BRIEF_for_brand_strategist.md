A **Career Intelligence Dashboard**: a live web app that turns Canadian labour-market data into
grounded career decisions. It's built end-to-end by me — data pipeline, AI layer, UX, evaluation.
It is **Phase 0: a proof-of-work**, not a scaled product. We say that openly; the honesty is the edge.

- **Live app:** https://career-intelligence-production.up.railway.app/
- **Code (public):** https://github.com/CREEDCONSULT/-career-intelligence-
- **My LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/mesomachukwu-mezie-akabudu-a2606b236/

## 2. Who I am / how to position me
**AI Product & Strategy Lead. Systems thinker. Research-driven builder. Toronto. (Creed Consult / Creed Mindshare.)**
Positioning in one sentence: **"I don't only use AI. I build systems with AI."**
The deeper belief to thread through everything: *to build the future, first deconstruct the architecture of the present.*

## 3. The single most important message
> **Career advice shouldn't just *sound* intelligent. It should be *grounded*.**
> **The model is not the source of truth. The system is.**

Everything ladders up to: **"AI as infrastructure, not AI as a feature."**
Campaign title: **AI as Infrastructure: Building My Career Intelligence System.**

- **Founders** → sees someone who turns an idea into a structured product + business model.
- **Product leaders** → sees problem decomposition, roadmap clarity, risk/trust awareness.
- **AI builders** → sees a genuinely grounded, evaluated LLM system.

| Fact | Value |

**The 8 modules:** Ask the Data · Career Advisor · Skill Demand · Salary Ranges · Role Fit ·
Resume Studio · Market Context · Market Brief.
**The system, plainly:** Data → Analytics → **Guarded AI** → UX → Evaluation → Productization.
**The signature technical idea (worth a whole post):** the AI writes a database query, the database
returns the numbers, and every figure in the answer is checked against the data — if it's not
supported, the system refuses. *A chatbot improvises. A product refuses.*

## 6. Voice & tone
Strategic, honest, builder-led. **Short paragraphs. Strong single-line statements. Contrast.** Explain
the thinking, never the hype. Sounds like a person who thinks deeply, not a corporate announcement.

**Rhythm to imitate:**
> I stopped thinking about AI as something I only ask questions.
> I started thinking about it as infrastructure.
> So I built a Career Intelligence Dashboard.

**❌ Never say:** "thrilled to announce," "revolutionary / game-changing," "AI-powered solution
changing the world," "fully automated career coach," "guaranteed outcomes," or any fake traction /
"users love it" without users. Never claim it's already scaled.
**✅ Lean on:** proof-of-work · data-grounded · AI as infrastructure · decision system · guarded AI
workflow · evaluation mindset · productization path · from research to product · the dashboard is the
wedge, the data layer is the moat.

## 7. Visual direction (must feel like the same world as the product)
**Mood:** premium dark dashboard · strategic intelligence console · research-lab-meets-product-strategy.
Clean, technical, executive, spacious — analytical but beautiful.
**Palette:** bg `#071018` · cards `#0B1520`/`#111C2A` · grid/border `#29384C` · **gold accent `#D5B56E`** ·
cream text `#F7F1E8` · slate `#AEB9C8` · data-blue `#7CB4FF` · verify-green `#8EE0B2` · warning-red `#FF9A9A` (rare).
**Type:** clean sans (Inter/Söhne) for titles; mono (JetBrains/Geist Mono) for numbers, labels, chips.
**Motifs:** faint grid overlay · one soft gold glow · thin gold divider under titles · rounded metric
cards · data→AI→decision flow arrows · numbered stage markers · a 6-node roadmap timeline · trust
scorecards · `PROOF-OF-WORK` / `● LIVE` pills.
**Hard bans:** no robots or humanoid AI · no neon cyberpunk/glitch/matrix · no cartoon startup blobs ·
no stock-photo people · no brain-with-chips clichés. *If it looks like generic "AI" stock art, it's wrong.*
**Best visual asset:** real screenshots of the app (Ask, Career Advisor, Resume Studio, Market Brief),
framed in a dark card with a thin border. Real frames beat any illustration.
