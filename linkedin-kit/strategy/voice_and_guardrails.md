# Voice, Guardrails & Build-in-Public Arc

## Voice in one paragraph
Strategic, honest, builder-led. Short paragraphs. Strong single-line statements. Contrast. Explain the
thinking, not the hype. Sound like a person who thinks deeply — not a corporate announcement. Confident
without overclaiming. The product refuses to invent numbers; the voice refuses to invent traction.

## Rhythm template (reuse this shape)
> I stopped [old frame].
>
> I started [new frame].
>
> So I built [the thing].
>
> The idea was simple:
> [principle, stated plainly].

---

## ❌ Do NOT say
- "I'm thrilled to announce…"
- "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "the future of job searching"
- "AI-powered solution changing the world"
- "Leveraging cutting-edge technology" (unless you immediately explain it practically)
- "I built this amazing AI tool"
- "This will revolutionize careers"
- "Fully automated career coach"
- "AI knows what job you should get"
- "This replaces career advisors"
- "Guaranteed career outcomes"
- Any metric that isn't real. Any "users love it" without users. Any claim it's already scaled.

## ✅ Preferred phrases (the campaign lexicon)

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable — this is the whole differentiator)
1. It's **Phase 0**. Say so. A proof-of-work with a roadmap, not a scaled product.
2. Every number is **real and reproducible from the repo**.
3. State limitations openly (e.g. "skills are inferred from titles + occupation, not full résumé text").
4. Refusals and imperfect scores are **features to show**, not flaws to hide.

## Build-in-Public narrative arc (the story spine under the 4 weeks)

Think of the campaign as a five-beat story. Each post advances one beat.

**Beat 1 — The reframe.** "I stopped using AI for answers and started building systems with it."
*Establishes the lens. (Week 1)*

**Beat 2 — The tension.** "Career advice sounds smart and is ungrounded. That gap bothered me."
*Creates the problem worth solving. (Week 1–2)*

**Beat 3 — The build.** "Here's how I designed it — as layers, not a prompt." Data → analytics → AI.
*Shows competence and structure. (Week 2)*

**Beat 4 — The proof.** "Here's the part most AI content skips: the guardrails and the evaluation."
*Earns trust with mechanism + real numbers. (Week 3)*

**Beat 5 — The horizon.** "It's Phase 0. Here's the ramp to a product — and who'd pay." 
*Turns a project into a trajectory. (Week 4)*

**Resolution (ongoing):** the Featured section + case study hold the story on autopilot; monthly
"build logs" (each Market Brief) keep it alive without a new campaign.

### The recurring line to keep planting
> AI strategy becomes real when it leaves the slide deck and turns into a working system.

If someone remembers one sentence from the whole campaign, make it that one.
