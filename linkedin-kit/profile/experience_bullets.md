## Bullets (choose 5–7)

- Designed and shipped an **end-to-end AI product** (Career Intelligence Dashboard) turning Canadian
  labour-market data into grounded career decisions — live app + open-source, 8 modules, 100 automated
  tests in CI.
- Built a **data layer** joining five official sources (Job Bank, Statistics Canada, Indeed Hiring
  Lab, Lightcast, NOC 2021) into one clean, queryable store — 97,227 Toronto postings, 5,148 skills,
  refreshed on a monthly automated schedule.
- Architected a **provider-agnostic, grounded AI layer**: natural-language questions are converted to

  structurally prevented from inventing figures.

  top-5 accuracy on test profiles), and grounded review/tailoring/cover-letter generation that never
  fabricates experience.
- Defined a **6-phase productization roadmap** (open demo → gated beta → personal intelligence layer →
  coach/institution dashboards → market expansion → B2B API), designed around a market-configurable
  engine that retargets to new cities via a single config.
- Ran the project as a **public build-in-public case study**, translating a systems-thinking method —
  deconstruct the system, build the workflow, automate the intelligence — into a shippable artifact.
