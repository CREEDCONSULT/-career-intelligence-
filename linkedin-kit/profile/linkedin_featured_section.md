## Short project case-study summary (for the link blurb / a pinned comment / recruiter DM)

**Career Intelligence Dashboard** — an end-to-end AI product that turns Canadian labour-market data
into grounded career decisions.

**The thesis:** career advice should be grounded, not guessed. The model is not the source of truth —
the system is.

**What I built:**
- A data layer joining five official sources (Job Bank, Statistics Canada, Indeed Hiring Lab,
  Lightcast, NOC 2021) into one clean, queryable store — 97,227 Toronto postings, 5,148 skills.
- A provider-agnostic AI layer that answers questions by writing validated SQL, running it, and
  narrating only the returned numbers — with a hard rule that it can never invent a figure.
- 8 product modules: Ask the Data, Career Advisor, Skill Demand, Salary Ranges, Role Fit, Resume
  Studio (upload → fit, review, tailoring, cover letter), Market Context, Market Brief.
- An evaluation loop with real numbers: 80% text-to-SQL accuracy, 0 invented numbers shown, 0.94
  brief faithfulness, 5/5 out-of-scope refusals, 8/8 resume-to-role matches.
- A phased productization roadmap from open demo to a B2B intelligence/API layer.

**Why it matters:** it's proof of how I work — research → systems → AI workflows → product → scale —
not a demo. Live app and full source are public.
