System architecture

Six layers. One rule.

The model lives in exactly one layer. Everything around it exists to make its answers trustworthy. That boundary is the product.

The stack

01

Data

Job Bank · Statistics Canada · Indeed Hiring Lab · Lightcast · NOC 2021 → one clean, queryable store (97,227 postings, 5,148 skills).

02

Analytics

Raw signals → skills in demand, salary ranges, role demand, hiring trends.

03

AI — guarded no-invent

Provider-agnostic gateway. Writes validated SQL, narrates only real results, refuses when unsupported.

04

UX

Eight modules — Ask, Career Advisor, Skill Demand, Salary Ranges, Role Fit, Resume Studio, Market Context, Market Brief.

05

Evaluation measured

Execution accuracy · faithfulness · refusal behaviour — real, reproducible numbers.

06

Productization

Open demo → gated beta → personal layer → coach/institution → expansion → API.

The one rule

The model does not get to invent a number.
